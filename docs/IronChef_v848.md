# 🏆 IRON CHEF: VERSION 848

## Two-Phase Ruleset (Final Form)

This keeps the experiment rigorous, fair, and legible.

---

# 🔍 PHASE A — RECIPE AUTHORING

*(Reverse-Engineering Round)*

### 🎯 Objective

Analyze **V1 (the finished dish)** and produce a **very intricate, execution-ready recipe prompt** capable of recreating the experience — *without writing the code*.

You are a **critic + recipe author**, not a builder.

---

## 📜 PHASE A RULES

### 1️⃣ No Implementation

* ❌ Do NOT write production code
* ❌ Do NOT refactor files
* ❌ Do NOT “start building”
* ✅ Analysis and documentation only

This phase ends with a **prompt artifact**, not a working app.

---

### 2️⃣ Analyze the Dish, Not the Ingredients

Focus on:

* pacing
* timing
* rituals (boot, echo, credits)
* intentional quirks
* emotional beats
* overengineering that adds *feel*

You are reverse-engineering **experience**, not architecture.

---

### 3️⃣ Extract Rules, Not Features

Avoid:

* “it has X file”
* “it uses Y pattern”

Instead document:

* “this always happens before that”
* “pauses feel intentional here”
* “this interaction hesitates, not snaps”
* “normalization would kill this moment”

---

### 4️⃣ Produce a Copy-Paste-Ready Recipe Prompt

Your output **must** include:

#### ✅ The Recipe Prompt

A single, structured prompt intended for a *fresh instance of yourself* to execute.

It must specify:

* goals
* constraints
* flavor philosophy
* non-negotiables
* acceptable deviations
* what NOT to optimize away

---

### 5️⃣ Define Acceptance Criteria

You must define:

* what “success” looks like
* parity signals
* taste checks
* stop conditions

If the executor meets these, they should stop.

---

### 6️⃣ Document the Process

Timeline entries are **mandatory**:

* 🔍 Observations
* 🧪 Hypotheses
* ⚠️ Uncertainty
* 📌 Decisions

Failure to document = penalty.

---

### 7️⃣ No Retroactive Editing

Once the recipe prompt is finalized:

* it is frozen
* no edits after execution begins

Bad recipes are valid outcomes.

---

# 🔧 PHASE B — RECIPE EXECUTION

*(Cook Round)*

### 🎯 Objective

Execute the provided recipe **faithfully**, while documenting progress, failures, and decisions under competition rules.

You are a **cook**, not a critic.

---

## 📜 PHASE B RULES

### 1️⃣ Follow the Recipe You Were Given

* No improvising unless instructed
* No silent deviations
* Any change must be logged and justified

If the recipe is flawed, **expose it**, don’t “fix it quietly.”

---

### 2️⃣ Preserve the Soul

Do NOT normalize away:

* quirks
* pauses
* rituals
* dramatic overengineering
* weird timing

Correct-but-bland = failure.

---

### 3️⃣ Commit Discipline

Judged on:

* frequency
* clarity
* checkpointing before risky changes
* reversions when needed

Hiding mistakes = penalty.

---

### 4️⃣ Timeline Documentation (Mandatory)

Log entries for:

* 🔧 Implementation
* ⚠️ Failures
* 🔁 Reverts
* ✨ Breakthroughs
* 📌 Decisions

Each entry must include:

* model/persona badge
* intent
* outcome

---

### 5️⃣ No Over-Engineering

Stop when:

* acceptance criteria are met
* parity is achieved
* further changes would be “gold-plating”

Restraint is part of the test.

---

## 🚫 DISQUALIFIERS (Both Phases)

Immediate disqualification if:

* documentation is skipped
* constraints are ignored
* soul is intentionally optimized away
* recipe is rewritten after execution begins

---
